# Instructor check before teaching

The student route has one question per lesson and no required technical choices. Explain the task, demonstrate one action, then let participants do it. Keep papers optional. Ask participants to point to a map, core interval or computed value when making a claim.

## A small real pilot

Invite 3–5 people unfamiliar with the materials, including participants who seldom use browser-based research software. Have them work simultaneously on international GeoGPT accounts. Do not silently rescue them or skip steps when timing.

Record for each lesson: account/Research access, download and upload time, prompt copying, service wait, clarification questions, opening every required figure, first evidence-based explanation, any helper intervention, and total lesson time. Record whether they can recover after a deliberate interruption by finding the last completed checkpoint.

A passing rehearsal requires all participants to reach the actual outputs, identify one source observation and one assumption, and make a supported recommendation within the class block. Any unexplained technical question is a defect in the instructions or defaults. Record the actual fastest and slowest times; do not label operator timings as novice timings.

Test enough simultaneous accounts to represent the intended class. Three successful individual runs do not establish service capacity. If the service fails, label prepared reference figures as reference figures rather than live results. Make a class fallback decision before teaching.

## Timing

Three 45-minute teaching blocks plus 15 minutes of orientation and transitions total 150 minutes. Video chapters are included in the blocks: pause and perform the action, then discuss its evidence. The five-minute computer target excludes registration and extensive reading; it is not a promise for every user or service condition.

## Scientific scope

Fossils: occurrence records and a selected historical age calibration constrain scenarios; the exercise does not discover a new observed unconformity. Treatise genus evidence is necessary but not sufficient for a species-level date.

Seismic: a controlled synthetic comparison supports a mapping decision about resolution; it does not establish a real reservoir's continuity.

Landslides: the equations from Maharjan et al. are implemented with explicit material and density-unit assumptions. New inputs and resolution mean this is not replication of the paper's validation. The negative relief coefficient criticized in the former regression lesson is absent. The present physical screen has no inventory performance claim.

The archived north/east held-out regression comparison remains useful as a separate advanced discussion about transferability. Its AUC values belong to a different model, grid and question; do not attach them to the present 30 m critical-acceleration maps.
