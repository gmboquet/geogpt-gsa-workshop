# One-prompt workshop: verification status

Each lesson now has six numbered steps, one supplied input and one required prompt. The account, upload, copy, paste, send and result-opening instructions are explicit. Technical defaults are supplied; no required paper reading, free-form technical answers, validation-purpose choices or morphology-key construction remain. Optional discussion and exploration are separate from completing the calculation.

All 6,920 Gorkha scores match independent scalar evaluation within 3.34e-16, with identical input rows, cell identifiers and coordinates. The final maps were opened in the actual Research interface. Gorkha square symbols show cell centres; they are not exact cell-area polygons. Scores are relative, not calibrated probabilities or confirmed failures.

Structural checks verify unique identifiers, working target references, embedded images, byte-identical input downloads and equality of standalone/combined lessons. No student forms or notebook viewer are required. Browser interaction checks are recorded separately after publication.

Earlier movies and handouts are preserved. Movies from the previous multi-prompt edition are not instructions for this new one-prompt route; no updated movie is claimed here.

## Participant acceptance remains open

The 45-minute lesson is a teaching plan: 10 minutes preparation, 5 minutes computer run, 10 minutes evidence inspection, 10 minutes discussion and 10 minutes exploration. It is not intentionally prolonged computer work. An actual first-time-user pilot is still required for all three lessons. An operator cannot establish that every novice or older participant will succeed unassisted. The supplied observer checklist requires no missing clicks, no unexplained choices, recovery after interruption and a plain-language explanation of one observation and one limitation.
