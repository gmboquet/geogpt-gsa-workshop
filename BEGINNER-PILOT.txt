FIRST-TIME USER ACCEPTANCE — ALL THREE LESSONS

Purpose
Test the lesson, not the participant. Do not describe age as ability. Include people unfamiliar with browser tabs, downloads and geology vocabulary.

Conditions
Use the public combined workshop link on the participant's own computer. Begin without a prepared GeoGPT project. Account registration is observed separately; it is not included in a five-minute signed-in operator target. Do not show the old multi-prompt videos as instructions for this one-prompt route.

Before starting
Ask: "Please follow this lesson as you would in class. Say what you are looking for. If a step is unclear, tell me. This tests our instructions, not you."
Do not teach the required click before the participant encounters its written instruction.

Record separately for Fossils, Seismic and Landslides
- Time entering the lesson, finding GeoGPT, finishing upload, sending prompt, receiving final output, opening output, and explaining it.
- Every missing button or different label, unintended tab closure, wrong-file selection, failed copy/paste, unexplained word, extra model question, and needed intervention.
- The exact instruction the learner had reached when stuck.
- Whether a saved example was mistaken for the learner's live result.
- Service delay separately from reading and navigation.

Pass criteria for each lesson
1. The participant completes the required sequence without the observer supplying a missing click, filename, scientific choice or technical answer. A lesson-provided recovery path may be used; record it.
2. The actual requested result exists. A code listing, planning text or old screenshot alone is not completion.
3. In ordinary words, the participant points to one observation and explains one limit. No specialist vocabulary test.
4. The participant can recover their place after a two-minute interruption without starting over.
5. At enlarged text size, instructions and controls remain readable and usable.
6. Required work and interpretation fit within 35 class minutes, leaving 10 minutes for questions/exploration. If they do not, revise or reduce scope; do not blame the learner.

Specific explanations
Fossils: a name needs visible internal evidence; unclear anatomy is not absent anatomy, and referral is legitimate.
Seismic: the rocks stay fixed while the pulse changes; a synthetic image is not proof about a real aquifer.
Landslides: higher modeled score is not confirmation of a landslide; blank means missing coverage.

Five-minute gate
A separate uninterrupted operator test starts at creating a fresh conversation/project with the account signed in and inputs already downloaded. Count upload, send, service wait and opening/checking the actual result. Keep the screen and wall-clock record. A model's thinking timer alone is not this measurement. Any preparation, recovery, expert correction or service overrun must be disclosed. One passing run is not a universal latency guarantee.

Release decision
No actual participant pilot has yet been recorded. Do not call the workshop beginner-validated or guarantee accessibility to every participant until this evidence exists. Record remaining failures even if the computational answer is correct.
