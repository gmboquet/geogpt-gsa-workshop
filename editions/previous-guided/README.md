# GeoGPT geoscience workshop

[Open the workshop website](https://gmboquet.github.io/geogpt-gsa-workshop/).

Three investigations: Treatise-based fossil identification, seismic thickness estimation, and geographic transfer of landslide prediction.

The fossil specimen viewer, observations and teaching materials are embedded in `workshop.html`. The two quantitative Research exercises use `workshop-runtime.zip`, or the individually accessible files under `runtime/`. Students use GeoGPT/Zero2x in a browser; calculations execute on its Research service. GitHub hosts the files, not the Python execution.

Start with the website's setup prompt in a new international Research project. Check `TEST-STATUS.md` for what has actually been rehearsed. Do not assume a successful download establishes successful execution.

The runtime manifest records every source and method hash. No credentials or existing private-project access are required by the distributed files. Source attribution and processing definitions are in `runtime/DATA-PROVENANCE.md` and the handout. Third-party materials retain their source attribution and terms.
