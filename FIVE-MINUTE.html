Nummulites: the description specifies an enrolled spiral sheet, with or without internal cavities; the illustrated median and transverse sections help interpret that coiled construction. Marginopora: a disc-shaped test with cyclic chambers, chamberlets connected by annular canals, and different main and lateral compartments.
